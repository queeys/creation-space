Your privacy.

This website prepares an enquiry message in your browser. It does not upload files or send form contents to a Creation Space server, and it does not save enquiry details in browser storage. Your browser may offer its own autofill features.

When you choose WhatsApp or email, the message is passed to that service or your email app. You must review and send it there. Those services handle information under their own terms and privacy policies. WhatsApp messages are placed in a link, which may be recorded in your browser history. Avoid sensitive or confidential information in initial enquiries.

This site does not include analytics, advertising trackers, or site cookies. GitHub Pages, as the hosting provider, may process technical request information such as IP addresses. See GitHub’s privacy statement ↗.

For questions about information you have sent, email creation.space@hotmail.com. Before launch, the owner should review this notice against the actual business practices.
